Free Caregiver Resources Directory

Free resources for family caregivers — respite care, support groups, benefit guides, and legal aid. Grouped by need, official links only.

Respite Care

National Respite Resource Center

The ARCH National Respite Resource Center (funded by the U.S. Administration for Community Living) is the national clearinghouse for respite care information, including a respite locator tool and a provider directory.

https://archrespite.org/

Finding local respite providers, learning about respite funding, and accessing toolkits for families.

National Respite Network

A membership association of respite providers and advocates working to expand respite services nationwide.

https://www.respitecare.org/

Connecting with state respite coalitions and learning about respite policy and training.

Family Caregiver Alliance (FCA)

A national nonprofit with educational resources for caregivers and a directory of caregiver support programs by state.

https://www.caregiver.org/

State-by-state caregiver resource guides and educational fact sheets on respite and caregiving strategies.

Caregiver Action Network (CAN)

A national nonprofit providing free educational resources and a family caregiver helpline.

https://caregiveraction.org/

Caregiver helpline (877-CAREGIVER), educational guides, and finding local support.

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Caregiver Support Program

For veterans and post-9/11 veterans, the VA offers respite care through the Caregiver Support Program, including in-home respite and temporary residential respite.

https://www.va.gov/family-member-benefits/caregiver-support/

Eligibility information, respite care details, and applying for VA caregiver support.

Eldercare Locator

A service of the U.S. Administration for Community Living that connects older adults and their families to local services, including respite care.

https://eldercare.acl.gov/

Support Groups

Family Caregiver Alliance (FCA) — Caregiver Online

FCA offers online support communities and educational webinars for family caregivers.

https://www.caregiver.org/online-support

Joining online caregiver communities and accessing free caregiver education.

Caregiver Action Network (CAN) — Support & Helpline

CAN runs a free national helpline (877-CAREGIVER) and connects caregivers to support resources.

https://caregiveraction.org/

Calling or chatting with trained staff for support and guidance.

Alzheimer's Association

A national nonprofit offering 24/7 helpline, online support communities, and in-person support groups for Alzheimer's and dementia caregivers.

https://www.alz.org/

24/7 helpline (800-272-3900), online support groups, and local chapter information.

National Alliance for Caregiving

A national coalition that researches caregiving and partners with organizations to support family caregivers.

https://www.caregiving.org/

Research, reports, and connecting to partner caregiver programs.

Caregiver Action Network — Caregiver Online Library

A free library of caregiver guides organized by condition and situation.

https://caregiveraction.org/library

Downloading free guides on specific caregiving topics.

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Caregiver Support Program

The VA offers peer support mentoring and monthly support groups for family caregivers of veterans.

https://www.va.gov/family-member-benefits/caregiver-support/

Benefit Guides

Medicare.gov

The official U.S. Medicare website, with guides to coverage, costs, and plans.

https://www.medicare.gov/

Understanding Medicare coverage for home health care and durable medical equipment.

Medicaid.gov

The official U.S. Medicaid website, with state-by-state program information.

https://www.medicaid.gov/

Learning about Medicaid long-term care and home- and community-based services.

Social Security Administration (SSA)

The official SSA site, with information on Social Security, SSI, and benefits for caregivers and dependents.

https://www.ssa.gov/

Applying for or managing Social Security and SSI benefits.

Eldercare Locator

A service of the U.S. Administration for Community Living that connects people to local benefits and services.

https://eldercare.acl.gov/

Finding local benefit programs and financial assistance by ZIP code.

Benefits.gov

The official U.S. government benefits portal, listing federal, state, and local benefit programs.

https://www.benefits.gov/

Searching for federal benefits by life event, including caregiving-related assistance.

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Benefits

The official VA site for veterans benefits, including disability compensation, pension, and dependent benefits.

https://www.va.gov/benefits/

Understanding veterans benefits that may support family caregivers.

211.org (United Way)

United Way's 211 service connects people to local health and human services, including utility and rent assistance.

https://www.211.org/

Legal Aid

Legal Services Corporation (LSC)

The nonprofit corporation established by Congress to support civil legal aid for low-income Americans. LSC funds state legal aid organizations.

https://www.lsc.gov/

Finding your state's legal aid organization through LSC's online directory.

National Legal Aid & Defender Association (NLADA)

The oldest and largest national organization dedicated to equal justice for low-income Americans.

https://www.nlada.org/

Connecting to legal aid programs and learning about civil legal aid.

AARP Legal Services

AARP offers legal services for members, including help with powers of attorney, advance directives, and caregiving-related documents.

https://www.aarp.org/legal/

Understanding legal document options and connecting to legal help.

National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys (NAELA)

A national professional association of attorneys specializing in elder law.

https://www.naela.org/

Finding an elder law attorney through NAELA's attorney directory.

U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Veterans Benefits Administration

The official VA site for veterans benefits and appeals.

https://www.va.gov/benefits/

Understanding veterans benefits and filing appeals.

Medicare.gov — Appeals

The official Medicare site with information on appealing coverage and payment decisions.

https://www.medicare.gov/claims-and-appeals/
