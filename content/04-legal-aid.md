# Legal Aid

Legal aid covers free or low-cost legal help for caregivers, including powers of attorney, advance directives, guardianship, and benefits appeals. Most organizations below offer free services or connect you to a legal aid office.

## Legal Services Corporation (LSC)
- **What it is:** The nonprofit corporation established by Congress to support civil legal aid for low-income Americans. LSC funds state legal aid organizations.
- **Official link:** https://www.lsc.gov/
- **Use for:** Finding your state's legal aid organization through LSC's online directory.

## National Legal Aid & Defender Association (NLADA)
- **What it is:** The oldest and largest national organization dedicated to equal justice for low-income Americans.
- **Official link:** https://www.nlada.org/
- **Use for:** Connecting to legal aid programs and learning about civil legal aid.

## AARP Legal Services
- **What it is:** AARP offers legal services for members, including help with powers of attorney, advance directives, and caregiving-related documents.
- **Official link:** https://www.aarp.org/legal/
- **Use for:** Understanding legal document options and connecting to legal help.

## National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys (NAELA)
- **What it is:** A national professional association of attorneys specializing in elder law.
- **Official link:** https://www.naela.org/
- **Use for:** Finding an elder law attorney through NAELA's attorney directory.

## U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Veterans Benefits Administration
- **What it is:** The official VA site for veterans benefits and appeals.
- **Official link:** https://www.va.gov/benefits/
- **Use for:** Understanding veterans benefits and filing appeals.

## Medicare.gov — Appeals
- **What it is:** The official Medicare site with information on appealing coverage and payment decisions.
- **Official link:** https://www.medicare.gov/claims-and-appeals/
- **Use for:** Learning how to appeal a Medicare decision.
