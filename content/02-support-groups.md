# Support Groups

Support groups let caregivers share experiences, coping strategies, and practical advice with others who understand what they are going through. Most are free or low-cost and meet in person, by phone, or online.

## Family Caregiver Alliance (FCA) — Caregiver Online
- **What it is:** FCA offers online support communities and educational webinars for family caregivers.
- **Official link:** https://www.caregiver.org/online-support
- **Use for:** Joining online caregiver communities and accessing free caregiver education.

## Caregiver Action Network (CAN) — Support & Helpline
- **What it is:** CAN runs a free national helpline (877-CAREGIVER) and connects caregivers to support resources.
- **Official link:** https://caregiveraction.org/
- **Use for:** Calling or chatting with trained staff for support and guidance.

## Alzheimer's Association
- **What it is:** A national nonprofit offering 24/7 helpline, online support communities, and in-person support groups for Alzheimer's and dementia caregivers.
- **Official link:** https://www.alz.org/
- **Use for:** 24/7 helpline (800-272-3900), online support groups, and local chapter information.

## National Alliance for Caregiving
- **What it is:** A national coalition that researches caregiving and partners with organizations to support family caregivers.
- **Official link:** https://www.caregiving.org/
- **Use for:** Research, reports, and connecting to partner caregiver programs.

## Caregiver Action Network — Caregiver Online Library
- **What it is:** A free library of caregiver guides organized by condition and situation.
- **Official link:** https://caregiveraction.org/library
- **Use for:** Downloading free guides on specific caregiving topics.

## U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Caregiver Support Program
- **What it is:** The VA offers peer support mentoring and monthly support groups for family caregivers of veterans.
- **Official link:** https://www.va.gov/family-member-benefits/caregiver-support/
- **Use for:** Peer support and connecting with other veteran family caregivers.
