# Benefit Guides

Benefit guides explain public and private programs that can help offset the cost of caregiving — including Medicare, Medicaid, Social Security, veterans benefits, and utility assistance. Most official sources are free.

## Medicare.gov
- **What it is:** The official U.S. Medicare website, with guides to coverage, costs, and plans.
- **Official link:** https://www.medicare.gov/
- **Use for:** Understanding Medicare coverage for home health care and durable medical equipment.

## Medicaid.gov
- **What it is:** The official U.S. Medicaid website, with state-by-state program information.
- **Official link:** https://www.medicaid.gov/
- **Use for:** Learning about Medicaid long-term care and home- and community-based services.

## Social Security Administration (SSA)
- **What it is:** The official SSA site, with information on Social Security, SSI, and benefits for caregivers and dependents.
- **Official link:** https://www.ssa.gov/
- **Use for:** Applying for or managing Social Security and SSI benefits.

## Eldercare Locator
- **What it is:** A service of the U.S. Administration for Community Living that connects people to local benefits and services.
- **Official link:** https://eldercare.acl.gov/
- **Use for:** Finding local benefit programs and financial assistance by ZIP code.

## Benefits.gov
- **What it is:** The official U.S. government benefits portal, listing federal, state, and local benefit programs.
- **Official link:** https://www.benefits.gov/
- **Use for:** Searching for federal benefits by life event, including caregiving-related assistance.

## U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Benefits
- **What it is:** The official VA site for veterans benefits, including disability compensation, pension, and dependent benefits.
- **Official link:** https://www.va.gov/benefits/
- **Use for:** Understanding veterans benefits that may support family caregivers.

## 211.org (United Way)
- **What it is:** United Way's 211 service connects people to local health and human services, including utility and rent assistance.
- **Official link:** https://www.211.org/
- **Use for:** Finding local financial, food, and utility assistance by ZIP code.
