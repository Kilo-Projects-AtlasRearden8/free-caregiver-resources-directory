# Respite Care

Respite care gives family caregivers a short, temporary break — from a few hours to a few days — so they can rest, run errands, or simply recharge. Respite can be provided at home, in a care facility, or through in-home aides. Many programs are funded through government grants and nonprofits; some are free or sliding-scale.

## National Respite Resource Center
- **What it is:** The ARCH National Respite Resource Center (funded by the U.S. Administration for Community Living) is the national clearinghouse for respite care information, including a respite locator tool and a provider directory.
- **Official link:** https://archrespite.org/
- **Use for:** Finding local respite providers, learning about respite funding, and accessing toolkits for families.

## National Respite Network
- **What it is:** A membership association of respite providers and advocates working to expand respite services nationwide.
- **Official link:** https://www.respitecare.org/
- **Use for:** Connecting with state respite coalitions and learning about respite policy and training.

## Family Caregiver Alliance (FCA)
- **What it is:** A national nonprofit with educational resources for caregivers and a directory of caregiver support programs by state.
- **Official link:** https://www.caregiver.org/
- **Use for:** State-by-state caregiver resource guides and educational fact sheets on respite and caregiving strategies.

## Caregiver Action Network (CAN)
- **What it is:** A national nonprofit providing free educational resources and a family caregiver helpline.
- **Official link:** https://caregiveraction.org/
- **Use for:** Caregiver helpline (877-CAREGIVER), educational guides, and finding local support.

## U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs — Caregiver Support Program
- **What it is:** For veterans and post-9/11 veterans, the VA offers respite care through the Caregiver Support Program, including in-home respite and temporary residential respite.
- **Official link:** https://www.va.gov/family-member-benefits/caregiver-support/
- **Use for:** Eligibility information, respite care details, and applying for VA caregiver support.

## Eldercare Locator
- **What it is:** A service of the U.S. Administration for Community Living that connects older adults and their families to local services, including respite care.
- **Official link:** https://eldercare.acl.gov/
- **Use for:** Finding local respite and other support services by ZIP code.
